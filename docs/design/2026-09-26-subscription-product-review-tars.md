g) Must-fix evidence/security contract:
• Replace "proves what it did" with "verifiable records of observed controls and actions". A signature authenticates bytes; it does not prove completeness, truthful instrumentation, successful prevention or universal agent coverage. A pack showing zero blocks may mean zero visibility.
• An external anchor commits a prefix THROUGH its sequence/head by receipt time. It does not prove the subsequent unanchored suffix or prevent suppressed events. Verify retained prefix against the receipt; detect forks, reset epochs, missing intervals and rollback to an older valid anchor. Retention checkpoints must preserve verifiable commitments and declare what can no longer be independently reconstructed. Crash/concurrent-writer, disk-full, audit-write-failure and migration tests are gating.
• The certificate's signed fields must include the device PUBLIC KEY (missing from the listed tuple), issuer/key ID, serial, org, purpose, scope and validity. Domain-separate licence/feed/cert/anchor/pack signatures; specify canonical encoding and strict schemas. Test roots must never be accepted as production trust.
• "Packs before revocation verify forever" needs trusted signing-time evidence and retained trust/revocation history. A compromised device key can backdate an unanchored pack. Offline verification must separate signature validity, time evidence, coverage integrity and revocation status as-of the bundled snapshot; it cannot know a future revocation.
• Signed feeds need freeze/expiry detection, engine/schema compatibility, safe key rotation, isolated regex evaluation with a killable budget, cumulative perf bounds and last-known-good atomic activation. Signatures and a finite FP corpus don't stop overblocking. Define emergency shipping versus the seven-day shadow window. A signed retraction must not silently erase an already-npm built-in protection.
• Remove broad autoApprove/enforce:false as the standard paid FP workaround (§5.4). It weakens the customer's controls; prefer narrow reviewed exceptions with expiry, scope and an audit entry. Doctor must report any resulting reduction.
• Specify tenant isolation, authenticated/idempotent anchors and billing webhooks, cert/key recovery, backup/restore, anchor retention/export and service shutdown continuity. Hash-only uploads still carry identifiers/timing metadata; redact before hashing/export and protect any low-entropy content hashes.
